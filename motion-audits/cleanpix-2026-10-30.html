CleanPix page: a strong hero, but the live picker snaps and one panel ignores reduced motion

This audit covers the CleanPix app page (/cleanpix), redesigned as a darkroom contact sheet with a live keeper picker. It looks at the hero entrance, the scroll reveals, the sample-scan board, the "how it works" panels and the closing band.

Overall

The hero is the best motion on the site: a masked two-line shout and a screenshot that lifts from the bottom edge, all CSS, all gated on reduced motion. Below the fold the page loses that care. The "how it works" panels slide their widths for 450ms on a layout property with no reduced-motion guard. The live picker, the page's whole idea, opens rows with a hard snap and gives frames a hover zoom that sticks on touch. And the page brings back the 24px reveal on headings and paragraphs that the site pass just removed.

Issues to address
CriticalJakubEmil

"How it works" panels slide their widths with no reduced-motion path

WhatAt 1024px and up, .cp-stage transitions flex-basis for 450ms. The rule sits outside any prefers-reduced-motion block, so a visitor who asked for less motion still gets five panels sliding across 1100px. It's also the only layout-property animation on the site: every frame reflows the row. On phones the panel text and screenshot appear with no transition at all.

Why it mattersLarge horizontal movement is a vestibular trigger, and reduced motion is a hard rule in this house style. Panels are switched by click and arrow keys; keyboard-initiated changes shouldn't animate across half the screen.

Recommended motionLet the widths snap, and move the motion to what's new: the opened panel's content fades in from 8px left over 240ms on the house ease-out, inside no-preference. Because .cp-stage-body goes from display: none to grid, a CSS animation on it replays on every open with no script, on every width. Transform and opacity only.

The live picker's rows snap open

WhatChoosing a group swaps .cp-detail from display: none to grid: four frames and the summary line appear in one frame, and the row below jumps down. The row's transition-colors is also dead once the reveal script runs, because the global .reveal-on [data-reveal] transition (unlayered) overrides the Tailwind utility; the pink fill snaps with JS and fades without it.

Why it mattersThis board is the page's whole idea. A hard cut on its main state change reads as unfinished, and the jump makes it hard to see which row just opened.

Recommended motionGive the opened detail a short arrival: opacity 0 → 1 and 6px down → 0 over 220ms, motion-safe only. Height still snaps (no height animation in this house style), but the eye now lands on the frames. Drop the dead transition-colors so the fill behaves the same with and without the script.

The page brings back the 24px reveal on headings and text

What.reveal-on .cp [data-reveal] doubles the site's calmed 12px travel to 24px, and data-reveal sits on every h2, intro paragraph and the privacy link. The site-wide motion pass removed exactly this: reveals on text, at 24px.

Why it mattersMotion on text only delays reading, and this page now moves differently from its three siblings. It's the uniform-fade-in and motion-on-static-text pattern from the anti-checklist.

Recommended motionRemove data-reveal from headings, paragraphs and the link; keep it on objects (board rows, the stage strip, feature tiles). Drop the 24px override so objects use the site's 12px; board rows keep their sideways direction at 12px.

Issues to address
ImportantEmilJakub

Every frame zooms on hover; nothing answers a tap

What.cp-frame:hover img { transform: scale(1.03) } runs on all 25 frames. The image has no clipping box, so the zoom grows past the 8px gutter and drags the keeper's inset outline with it. On touch, :hover sticks to the last tapped frame, so it stays zoomed. A tap itself gets no feedback. And the intended colour preview on hover never shows: .cp-frame:hover img (0,2,1) loses to the grey-out rule .cp-frame:has(input:not(:checked)) img (0,2,2), so only the zoom is visible.

Why it mattersPicking a frame is the most repeated action on the page. Hover zoom across a grid is the hover-scale-on-everything pattern; what a repeated pick needs is a quick press, not a zoom.

Recommended motionDrop the hover zoom; make the colour preview actually win (scope it under .cp-board) and limit it to (hover: hover) devices. Add a press: :active scales the frame to 0.97 over 120ms ease-out, the same press the site uses on buttons.

On a phone, the Play button waits 1.1s

What.cp-after (the sentence and the Play button) rises over 600ms after a 500ms delay. On phones it sits right under the shout, so the page's primary action is invisible for half a second and only settles at 1.1s.

Why it mattersThe shout is legible by ~300ms on its expo-out curve; making the action wait another 800ms is pure tax on every visit.

Recommended motionStart it at 250ms and run it for 500ms: it still follows the headline, but settles at 0.75s. Leave the picture's lift as it is; it is decoration and doesn't hold anything back.
