Background Wizard page — calm swaps, fewer fades, nothing that snaps

A pass over the /background-wizard app page after its taste redesign: the sticky before/after tile that swaps tools as you scroll, the tool tabs, the drag knob, the scroll reveals and the FAQ — read through three motion lenses.

Overall

The bones are right: the photo is on screen at first paint, the hero rise is CSS-only and guarded, and the before/after uses clip-path. What lets it down is finish — the tool swap dims through the teal field, the active tab's label blinks out mid-fade, the FAQ snaps open — and volume: 29 elements fade up on scroll, most of them plain text. Fix the three seams, cut the reveals to the pictures, and the page reads as calm rather than busy.

02

Where the timings land

Nothing is sluggish. The issues are the two hollow dots — state changes with no transition — and the volume of deliberate-zone reveals on text.

The animations

  1. 1Hero .rise (+50ms stagger ×4)500ms
  2. 2Scroll reveal, 29 elements (+≤300ms stagger)400ms
  3. 3Tool pair crossfade350ms
  4. 4.press tap, tab fill, FAQ chevron150ms
  5. 5Active-tab label colournone
  6. 6FAQ answer opennone
Instant / Responsive · 0–300ms — taps, tabs, hovers, small state changes.
Deliberate · 300–500ms — fine for a hero and a large photo swap; costly when it gates reading.
Sluggish · 500ms+ — nothing here.
What's off

Durations are fine. Dot 2 is the cost: a 400ms fade on every heading, paragraph and FAQ row means the reader waits on text. Dots 5 and 6 are seams with no transition where their neighbours have one.

ImportantJakub

The tool swap dims through the teal field

WhatEach tool's before/after pair is stacked and swapped by toggling .is-on; the outgoing pair fades out while the incoming fades in, both over 350ms with stock ease-out. Mid-swap both are near half opacity, so the dark --bw-field shows through and the photo visibly dims.

Why it mattersThis tile is the page's centrepiece and swaps every time a tool scrolls into view. A brightness dip reads as a reload, not a change of tool on the same photo — the exact story the page tells.

Recommended motionLayer it: the incoming pair sits on top and fades in over 300ms on the site curve; the outgoing pair holds at full opacity and hides only after the incoming one is opaque (transition-delay). Opacity only.

The active tab's label blinks out while its fill fades

WhatTool tabs use the shared .press class, which transitions transform and background-color but not color. When a tab turns on, its text flips to canvas instantly while the fill is still canvas — for ~100ms the label is invisible. Turning off does the reverse.

Why it mattersThese tabs change on every scroll past a tool section, so the blink repeats. It is small but it's the kind of flicker people feel without naming.

Recommended motionMove fill and label together: add color to the tabs' transition (page-local, same 150ms as .press).

FAQ answers snap open

WhatOpening a <details> fades its background (150ms) and rotates the chevron (150ms), but the answer itself pops in with no transition — the one part the visitor came for is the one part that doesn't move.

Why it mattersHalf-animated state changes read as unfinished. The fix doesn't need height animation.

Recommended motionOn open, the answer fades in with a 4px drop over ~220ms (opacity + transform only, under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference). Close stays instant — exits should be quieter than enters.

ImportantEmilJakub

29 elements fade up on scroll, most of them text

WhatEvery heading, paragraph, the CTA link, all six FAQ rows, the step tiles and both photos carry data-reveal — identical 400ms opacity + 12px rise, with a stagger on the FAQ list and the steps grid. That is the uniform-fade and stagger-on-every-list pattern.

Why it mattersReading waits on motion, and when everything moves nothing stands out. Text should be there when you arrive; pictures can earn an entrance.

Recommended motionKeep data-reveal only on image blocks: the cut-out, the three step tiles (one stagger moment), the closing photo. Drop it from headings, paragraphs, the CTA link, the FAQ rows and the story copy.

ImportantEmil

On phones the scroll sync changes a tile nobody can see

WhatThe IntersectionObserver that picks the tool from the text in view runs at every width. Below lg the compare tile isn't sticky — it has scrolled off the top — so the swaps happen off-screen and quietly undo the tab the visitor tapped.

Why it mattersMotion nobody sees is cost with no meaning, and state that changes behind your back breaks trust: scroll back up and the tool you picked is gone.

Recommended motionOnly follow the text while the tile is sticky (min-width: 1024px). On phones the tabs alone choose the tool.

ImportantEmil

The drag knob moves on left

WhatEvery input event writes --pos; the knob reads it as left: var(--pos), so each drag frame runs layout on the knob as well as repainting the two clip-paths.

Why it mattersThis is the page's one direct-manipulation surface; it should track the finger on the compositor, especially on low-end Android phones — the app's audience.

Recommended motionMake the knob a full-width layer moved with transform: translateX(var(--pos)) (percent of its own full width = percent of the tile). Clip-paths stay as they are.
