CirclePix page: a great headline entrance, but the style picker snaps

This audit covers the CirclePix app page (/circlepix), redesigned as colour chapters around the round crop. It looks at the hero entrance, the style picker, the scroll reveals, the press and hover states, and the looping app footage. Each part is read through three motion lenses, weighted for an app's marketing page.

Overall

The headline is the best motion on the site: both lines surface through a mask, and the paper copy above the waterline moves with them, so the text rises out of the coral sea. Everything else is less considered. The page's one live thing, the style picker, swaps both photos with no transition at all. The page fades up its headings and copy, which the site audit asked to stop. The hero circle enters exactly like the brand label. And the looping footage can only be paused by visitors who turned motion off.

Issues to address
CriticalJakubJhey

The style picker swaps both photos with no transition

WhatPicking Ring, Fill, Neon, #OPENTOWORK or Two-tone flips .cp-pair from display: none to block through :has(:checked). The input photo and the finished circle change in the same frame, with no fade.

Why it mattersThis is the page's one live thing, and it's the "result reveal" a photo app should be proud of. An instant swap of two large images reads as a glitch, and the eye can't tell the new circle from the old one. This is a motion gap in primary UI, which the checklist rates critical.

Recommended motionBecause the pair goes from display: none to block, a CSS animation on .cp-pair replays on every pick with no script. The result circle fades in from scale(0.96), and the input photo only fades. Both run 220ms on the site's ease-out. There is no exit: the old pair just leaves, so arrow-key scrubbing through the radios still feels instant. With reduced motion, keep the instant swap.

ImportantJakubEmil

22 elements share one fade-up, including every heading, the copy and the picker

Whatdata-reveal sits on all four h2s, the closing paragraph and CTA row, the five picker labels, the four progress stages, seven board tiles, the input photo and the video. They all get the same 12px / 400ms rise. Where several arrive together, they stagger 50ms apart.

Why it mattersThe site-wide audit settled that text should not move ("no motion on text; objects rise"). This page brought the reveal back to every heading. The five picker labels are controls, and fading them in one by one makes the chooser look like it's loading. With everything moving the same way, nothing stands out.

Recommended motionRemove data-reveal from the h2s, the closing paragraph, the CTA row and the five picker labels. They are simply there. Keep the rise on the objects: the input photo, the progress stages, the board tiles and the video.

Issues to address
ImportantJheyJakub

The hero circle enters like the brand label, on a different curve from the headline

WhatThe round picture is the first thing people see. It uses the shared .rise: 12px up with a fade, 500ms on cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), the same as the "CirclePix" label and the CTA band. The headline next to it surfaces over 900ms on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1).

Why it mattersTwo curves in one moment make the hero land in two halves: the circle has settled while "your ring" is still rising. The page is about the round crop, yet its circle is treated like a label.

Recommended motionGive the circle its own entrance on the headline's curve: from scale(0.92) and transparent to full size, 900ms on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), starting with line two (110ms). The ring and the word "ring" then arrive together. This uses scale rather than transform, so it composes with the circle's -translate-y-1/2. It stays transform/opacity only, and reduced motion leaves it static.

Issues to address
ImportantEmilJakub

The looping footage can only be paused with reduced motion on

WhatThe closing video is muted loop autoplay. Controls are only added when the visitor already has prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. For everyone else it plays forever with no way to stop it, on touch or keyboard.

Why it mattersWCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) asks for a pause on any moving content that starts by itself and lasts more than 5 seconds. Most people with motion sensitivity have never found the OS setting. The site audit fixed the same gap on the marquee.

Recommended motionAlways render native controls, trimmed with controlslist="nodownload noplaybackrate" and disablepictureinpicture. Browsers hide the bar while the clip plays and show it on hover or tap, so the look stays the same. The reduced-motion script then only needs to pause and drop autoplay.
